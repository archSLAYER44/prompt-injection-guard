======================================================================
7. END TO END, IN FIVE LINES
======================================================================

1. The text is normalized: Unicode cleaned, leetspeak and look-alike letters decoded, base64, hex
   and rot13 runs replaced by what they decode to.
2. It is cut into pieces: overlapping windows of up to 126 tokens, each sentence alone, and any
   decoded hidden text as its own reading.
3. Each piece becomes 404 numbers: a 384-number multilingual sentence embedding plus 20 rule counts,
   such as override phrases, role changes, exfiltration requests, dangerous commands and tool orders.
4. A linear SVM scores every piece, and the text takes the score of its most suspicious piece.
5. A score above 0.548 is the verdict "injection", otherwise "benign", reported with a confidence
   from the distance to that threshold and the list of rules that fired.
