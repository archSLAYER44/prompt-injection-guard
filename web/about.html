Injection Guard: how it works and where it stops

One text in, a verdict out: injection or benign, with a score, a confidence and the rules that fired. A sentence embedding plus twenty hand-written rule features into a linear SVM. No LLM call inside the detector, nothing executed, offline after the first download. It is a first filter, to be combined with limits on what the assistant may do.
